## A Provisioning Rulebook That Rewrites the Credit Desk

On 27 April 2026, the RBI issued final expected credit loss directions for scheduled commercial banks, effective 1 April 2027. Provisioning no longer waits for a loan to turn sour. Banks must estimate losses ahead of default, using staged classification and models built on default probability, loss severity and exposure.

The transition impact can be spread across four financial years, but the groundwork cannot wait. Data lineage, model governance and validation all need owners today, and Delhi NCR, where large lenders run their corporate credit and policy teams, feels the pressure first.

## Reading a Credit Risk Resume Without Being Fooled

Designations tell you little; context tells you everything. Ask which portfolio, what ticket size, and which phase of the credit cycle the candidate actually handled. Someone who managed corporate exposures through a downturn reads very differently from someone who only worked in benign years. Look for ownership of numbers such as slippage trends, override rates, and validation findings closed.

Confirm that a claimed model was built by the candidate, not merely used. A conversation with a former reporting manager often reveals more than any certificate. Since notice periods in banks run long, a plan for the gap, including interim ownership and handover notes, matters as much as the offer letter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Which credit risk roles are most in demand in Delhi banks?

Modelling and validation specialists, early warning leads and credit policy heads are in highest demand. Provisioning reforms, stressed unsecured portfolios and MSME monitoring requirements are pushing banks to prioritise people who can quantify risk, challenge assumptions and explain results confidently to auditors and senior management.

### Q2. Is prior expected credit loss experience mandatory?

No, it is not mandatory, though it is a strong advantage. Banks often accept candidates with solid credit modelling, scorecard, or stress testing backgrounds, provided they learn staging logic quickly. Demonstrated judgement on real portfolios usually outweighs a perfect keyword match on a resume.

### Q3. Can NBFC or fintech lending professionals move into bank credit risk?

Yes, they can, particularly when their experience covers portfolio analytics, collections strategy, or underwriting model development. Banks value the speed and data fluency these candidates bring, though they will test governance exposure, regulatory reporting familiarity, and comfort with committee-driven decisions.

### Q4. Does an assignment test matter for senior credit roles?

Yes, it matters a great deal. A practical case on a stressed account or model assumption reveals reasoning that resumes hide. It protects employers from title inflation, and it gives strong candidates a fair chance to prove depth beyond what an interview conversation alone can show.
